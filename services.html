FAQ

Questions clients ask us

What does Anvira Labs do?

Anvira Labs is a software development and technology consulting company from Gurugram, India. We build websites, web and mobile apps, SaaS platforms and AI features, run AWS cloud and DevOps, and provide fractional CTO support.

Where is Anvira Labs based, and who do you work with?

We are based in Gurugram (Delhi NCR), India, with a presence in Brampton, Ontario, Canada. We work with startups and growing businesses in India, Canada and the US, remotely or on site.

Can you add AI or a chatbot to my existing product?

Yes. We add LLM and RAG features such as chat and search over your own content, support assistants and content automation, usually as a 4–6 week sprint from pilot to production, with guardrails and cost controls.

Can you reduce our AWS or cloud bill?

Yes. Our 2–4 week fixed-fee cloud audit reviews your infrastructure, CI/CD and monitoring, and ends with a 30/60/90-day plan. Our founder has cut AWS costs by 22% while keeping 99.95% uptime in a previous role.

What is a fractional CTO, and do you offer one?

A fractional CTO is a senior technology leader who works with you part-time, typically 1–2 days a week, to own architecture, hiring and delivery without the cost of a full-time executive. Anvira Labs offers this to founders in India, Canada and the US.

How do we start a project with Anvira Labs?

Book a free 30-minute call on Calendly or send the contact form. We reply within one business day, then agree a clear scope, timeline and price before any code is written.
