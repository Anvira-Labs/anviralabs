How we work

A simple process, built for clarity

  1. 01

    Discover

    A free 30-minute call, then a short review of your product, systems and goals to find the highest-value starting point.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A clear scope, architecture, timeline and price before any code is written. No open-ended hourly surprises.

  3. 03

    Build

  4. 04

    Support
