About Anvira Labs

A family-run technology company, built by engineers

Anvira Labs is an Indian company co-founded by Vipan Kumar and Ritika Angrish to give growing businesses the kind of senior engineering usually reserved for large companies.

Most businesses don't need another slide deck. They need someone who has run an engineering team, owned the cloud bill and fixed production at 2am, and who will build the thing, not just recommend it.

We're a small, senior team based in Gurugram (Gurgaon), India, with a presence in Brampton, Ontario, Canada. We work with startups and established companies on websites, apps, cloud platforms and AI, and we stay accountable for how what we build performs in production.

Being small is deliberate: you talk to the people doing the work, decisions are fast, and nothing gets lost between sales and delivery.
