## What they ALL do (the conversion formula)

1. **One-sentence positioning above the fold** — who I help + with what + the outcome. Never "Welcome to my site".
2. **Photo + first-person voice** ("Hey I'm Stephan", "Hi folks"). Friendly, not corporate. The person is the brand.
3. **A credibility line immediately under the headline** — 3–5 hard numbers + 2–4 recognisable names. Years · people/teams · $ or % results · logos · book/talk.
4. **Name the buyer and the pain explicitly** — "non-technical founders", "fast-growing cos outpacing eng", "prepping for due diligence", "investors lost confidence". The reader should self-select in 5 seconds.
5. **Offerings framed as outcomes, with engagement model visible** — "1–2 days/week", "4-week fixed-scope audit", "starts at 1 day/wk, scales to 2–3". Ranges/pricing optional but the *shape* of an engagement is always shown.
6. **One primary CTA, repeated** — almost always "Book a free 30-min call" (low risk, explicit duration) + a lower-friction secondary (newsletter / free checklist / health score).
7. **Anti-sales framing to lower risk** — "I'll tell you whether this makes sense — even if the answer is 'not me'", "I only take 2 engagements/yr", "not here to make myself indispensable".
8. **Proof placed where doubt arises** — testimonials next to offerings, logos near the top, quantified results in the services section.
9. **Résumé-style career ladder on the About page** — current role → notable past companies → founded things → books/talks → education last. Reads like a CV but with impact numbers, not duties.
10. **Persistent "social proof engine"** — newsletter + LinkedIn + blog/talks. Content is the trust builder between visits.
